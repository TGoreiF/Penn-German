About the sound and spelling

No phone or computer has a Pennsylvania Dutch voice. The app uses an English or German computer voice as a rough guide, so it will not sound native.

For the real sound, tap 🎙 under any word and record a native speaker. That recording then plays whenever you tap Hear, in lessons, flashcards and the quiz. It stays on this device.

Spelling varies between communities. Most words follow the Deitsh spelling used in jw.org/pdc; a few everyday greetings use a more German-looking spelling. Ask a native speaker to confirm anything before you rely on it. Starter vocabulary was gathered from the learn-dutch.org word list, Omniglot and jw.org/pdc.
